# Montra

Montra is an independent mobile-first personal and shared expense app.

Authoritative implementation scope and product decisions are in
[the product blueprint](../docs/PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md). Setup and migration requirements are
in [deployment](../docs/DEPLOYMENT.md). The first foundation uses Expo/React Native,
FastAPI, MongoDB, direct Google/Apple authentication and direct Gemini receipt extraction.

Shared-account collaboration, budgets and offline synchronization remain planned work;
current contacts and groups are private to their creator.
