
## Implemented shared-group milestone

The Together tab now connects to shared membership and a group ledger: create/join groups, owner-managed invitation codes, equal/exact/percentage/ratio splits, payer selection, member balances, suggested repayments, recorded payments, and correction history. Existing private contact presets remain separate and accessible. This milestone is not a production-release declaration: native testing, live authentication, real database concurrency validation, and the remaining roadmap still apply.
